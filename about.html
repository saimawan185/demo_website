Our story

Neighbourhood care with specialist depth.

Derma Care was set up so residents of I-8 Markaz and nearby sectors would not need to travel far for a proper dermatology consult. The clinic combines medical skin care with laser and aesthetic services, so you can handle acne, pigmentation and rejuvenation questions in one calm visit to Ahmed Centre.

Assoc. Prof. Dr. Dur e Shehwar Sikandar leads care with an MCPS and MD background plus AAAM training in aesthetics. Her style is patient-first: listen carefully, explain the diagnosis in plain Urdu or English, and only recommend peels, lasers or injectables when they clearly help. That approach shows in her 4.8 verified rating and the satisfaction patients share on Marham.

Whether you need a routine check for stubborn acne, melasma follow-up, laser hair removal counselling or anti-aging options such as fillers, HIFU or thread lift, the team aims for a clear visit — ground-floor access, WhatsApp booking, and follow-up on the same number after treatment.

We also welcome honest feedback after your appointment. Reviews help other families in Islamabad choose confidently — and help us keep improving the clinic experience at I-8.
