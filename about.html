An independent Katoomba practice listed for general dentistry — not a national brand.

Dr Derek C. Davidoff

BDS · General dental surgeon

Dr Davidoff is listed as a general dentist serving Katoomba from 143 Katoomba Street. Public directories record the surgery under his name, with bookings by telephone only — there has not been an official clinic website until this demonstration.

The work is everyday restorative and family dentistry: keeping teeth comfortable, repairing what can be saved, and referring when specialist care is the better path.
