About Dentalogix

A modern specialist practice in Bank Square Market, Model Town.

Model Town, Lahore

Care that feels specialist, not rushed.

Dentalogix is a well-equipped dental clinic known for trained consultants, modern technology, and a calm patient experience.

Patients visit for braces, oral surgery, cosmetic dentistry, and everyday family care. The clinic sits on the 3rd floor of 72-77 Bank Square Market — easy to reach from across Model Town and nearby neighbourhoods.

Typical consultation fees range from Rs. 1,000 to Rs. 1,500, and doctor timings can vary by specialist.
